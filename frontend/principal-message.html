Principal's Message

Industries have very important and vital role in the country's growth, economy and prosperity and so the development of whole society is termed as socio-economic development. Even, for agricultural side, proper industrial environment is absolutely necessary for getting higher and higher yield towards achieving the status of surplus food stuff of the country.

To achieve such goal, the technical workforce of the country must be educated to attain skill in their respective field with proper sincerity and devotion. Quality is a vital issue to bring the system to the forefront globally.

Huge industrial growth with new industries to come up and expansion of existing industries are expected in near future resulting in the demand for quality training and skilled workforce will surge accordingly.

Our I.T.I being governed by N.C.V.T. guideline is established with the objective of delivering world class workforce of technician through our classroom lectures and shop floor training programme by our qualified and experienced faculties and yield the best results for the industry's quality training. Our entire team of faculties is committed to shape the students for industries and help them stand in a strong technical platform.

Even with little academic qualification, student may come to join us for being a complete technical personnel with fundamental qualities of technician in theory and practice.

I wish to have whole-heated and active cooperation in this respect from guardians, teachers and well-wishers towards fulfillment of our goal and objectives of our institution.

Let the students come forward to reap the benefits of R.A SHAW PVT ITI — a right place to build the career.

Principal
