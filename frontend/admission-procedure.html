Admission Procedure

  • Admission is open to candidates from all districts of West Bengal and to other state candidates residing in West Bengal.
  • Candidates must meet the prescribed physical standards and provide a medical certificate.
  • Additional weightage will be given to candidates with higher qualifications.
  • Selected candidates will be informed by post or phone to take admission by the due date.
  • At the time of admission, candidates must submit all original certificates including Madhyamik or Class VIII mark sheet, school/college leaving certificate, blood group certificate, and one set of Xerox/photo copies, along with 6 passport size photographs.
  • Admission is not guaranteed and may be refused by the principal without assigning any reason.
  • No extension of time will be allowed after the fixed date of admission.
  • Reservations of seats will be made according to government guidelines for reserved categories, considering academic standards and the number of applications received.
