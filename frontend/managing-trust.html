» RAM AWADH SHAW MEMORIAL TRUST

The R. A. Shaw Private Industrial Training Institute is the brain child of Sri Shyam Narayan Jaiswal (An entrepreneur in industry and an educationist), President / Secretary of "Ram Awadh Shaw Memorial Trust".

The R.A. Shaw Pvt. I.T.I. has been established by the trust and is located in a semi-urban area, Andal, Dist. - Paschim Bardhaman, West Bengal, PIN-713321.

This institute is established in an eco-friendly surrounding, easily approachable by land and rail-route from the areas of Durgapur, Asansol, Raniganj, and the district headquarters at Burdwan.
