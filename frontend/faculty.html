FACULTY

Highly Qualified & Experienced Faculty

This institute is run by a highly qualified and experienced Principal (B.E, M.Tech Mechanical Engineering) with 26 years of industrial experience in big industries like MAMC, ALSTOM, and about 12 years as faculty in technical institutions like Gov I.T.I, N.I.T, N.P.T.I, and many reputed private engineering colleges and training centers.

The faculties, qualified with B.Tech in Engineering, Diploma in Engineering, MCA, CTI, ITI, N.C.V.T, and graduates with sufficient industrial exposure, are engaged and inducted for sustaining and maintaining a quality training program. This ensures students feel immersed in an industrial environment from the very beginning of their training.
