The R.A Shaw Private Industrial Training Institute is the brain of Sri Shyamnarayan Jaswal,President/Secretary,
Ram Awadh Shaw Memorial Trustee Board. The R. A. Shaw Pvt I.T.I has been established by the Ram Awadh Shaw
Memorial Trust and is conveniently located in a semi-urban area, Andal, Dist.- Paschim Bardhaman, West Bengal
Pin-713321. This institute is established in an Eco-friendly surrounding, easily approachable by land & Rail-route
from the areas of Durgapur, Asansol, Raniganj and the district headquarters at Burdwan.
It is approximately 160 km from the capital city of Kolkata by rail and 180 km byroad.It is about 12 km from Durgapur
City Center. Durgapur, an industrial city that nurtures Durgapur Steel Plant (DSP) and Alloy steels Plant of SAIL, a
number of govt. and private industries in power and other sectors,educational and a good number of technical
institutions. it is 10 km away from Raniganj,one of oldest towns and a coal belt area and about 25 km away from
Asansol. it is well connected by Indian Railway (Andal Railway Station)and Andal Airport (Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport),
road i.e national highway(NH-2).
This I.T.I is set up in such an industrial and resourceful environment that the students passing out from this institute
are supposed to be flooded with jobs.But without skill development,the dream will not be transmitted into reality.
Apart from thousands of small entrepreneurs in industry,this institute is surrounded by Mega Steel Plants ,D.S.P,
A.S.P and other private industries viz. Alstom, PCBL,Graphite India, Balaji etc. In the east and IISCO in burnpur,
McNally Bharat, Dishergarh power plant in the west, Mega and super power plants like DVC, NTPC ,DPL ,DTPS,
Bakreswar TPS, Maithon Hydel Power etc. in the north and south.A new airport,adjacent to this institute will shortly
be in operation. We need not require to mention the existence of huge deposit of coal,the "Black Diamond"
underground near its immediate and nearest surrounding.The demand of skilled manpower is required everywhere
in these areas.So job prospects are definitely noteworthy surrounding this institute.
The institute aims at imparting training both theoretically and practically to the student of ordinary merit including school
dropouts in various job-oriented courses in commercial and technical discipline so that their skill development initiative
through this institute confidently put themselves in service in a well-established future by securing a job in government or
private industries/establishments or through entrepreneurship development process.
