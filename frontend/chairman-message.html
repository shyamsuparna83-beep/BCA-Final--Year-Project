From the Chairman’s Desk

Let me welcome you to our Fold!

In tune with the times, the primary target of this institute is to develop technical knowledge among the trainees as per the schedule course governed by N.C.V.T., Govt. of India and keeping space with rapidly changing industrial and world scenario, it has been our enduring endeavor to achieve consistent high quality education and thus produce in the long run high skilled technician personnel and offering them the best platform for suitable induction in Industry as per industrial need.

Both the State Government and the Central Government are now focusing on more and more industrial work force to come-up which would be in need in very near future for an economic development through industrialization.

In the present days of globalization, it is quite sure that job market is on flourishing with requirement of the technically qualified persons and the job opportunity in the market in near future will come to the door step of those who have acquired the real technical skill through training process at I.T.I.s. We always keep our vision clear towards our ultimate objective and have taken the task to shape the world skilled hands.

Students are most welcome to join us to reach their goal.

Let us hope for the best in future around.

Sincerely,
Chairman
